Legend:
  .  floor
  ~  water
  (space)  solid ground outside the level (impassable)
  @  door
  R  trader
  *  crystal
  /  mirror, glass from bottom-left to top-right

Heroes:
  trader #4 at (15,18), group 1
  trader #5 at (17,18), group 2
Objects:
  crystal #0 at (17,20)
  magic mirror #2 at (15,20), facing north (reflects toward south and east)
Doors: (14,29), (15,17)

Moves: U D L R move the selected hero group north, south, west, east; C selects the next group; X activates the magic mirrors.
