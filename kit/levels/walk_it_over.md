# walk_it_over

Size 82 x 60. Heroes: trader. Mechanics: mirrors, water.
Goal: every hero stands on a door at the same time (as many heroes as doors).
In this level a hero standing above layer 0 (for example on a wall) may step out over water. From layer 0, heroes still refuse to step into water.

Legend:
  .  floor
  ~  water
  (space)  solid ground outside the level (impassable)
  @  door
  R  trader
  *  crystal
  \  mirror, glass from top-left to bottom-right

Heroes:
  trader #1 at (44,28), group 1
  trader #7 at (45,31), group 2
Objects:
  magic mirror #2 at (44,29), facing east (reflects toward west and south)
  crystal #3 at (45,27)
  crystal #4 at (45,28)
  crystal #5 at (45,29)
  crystal #6 at (45,30)
Doors: (27,17), (53,17)

Moves: U D L R move the selected hero group north, south, west, east; C selects the next group; X activates the magic mirrors.
