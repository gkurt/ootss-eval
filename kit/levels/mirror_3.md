  o  boulder

Heroes:
  trader #2 at (23,21), group 1
Objects:
  magic mirror #0 at (15,17), facing north (reflects toward south and east)
  boulder #1 at (21,21)
  boulder #3 at (23,10)
  boulder #4 at (22,12)
Doors: (27,16)

Moves: U D L R move the selected hero group north, south, west, east; C selects the next group; X activates the magic mirrors.
