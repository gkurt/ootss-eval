# island_hopping

Size 30 x 26. Heroes: warrior, wizard. Mechanics: water.
Goal: every hero stands on a door at the same time (as many heroes as doors).

Legend:
  .  floor
  #  wall
  ~  water
  (space)  solid ground outside the level (impassable)
  @  door
  W  warrior
  Z  wizard
  *  crystal

Heroes:
  wizard #0 at (7,15), group 2
  warrior #1 at (9,4), group 1
Objects:
  crystal #2 at (15,21)
  crystal #3 at (21,17)
  crystal #4 at (5,13)
  crystal #5 at (23,7)
  crystal #6 at (4,14)
  crystal #9 at (24,8)
  crystal #10 at (13,10)
Doors: (15,12), (14,12)

Moves: U D L R move the selected hero group north, south, west, east; C selects the next group.
