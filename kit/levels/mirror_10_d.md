# mirror_10_d

Size 42 x 42. Heroes: trader. Mechanics: mirrors, water.
Goal: every hero stands on a door at the same time (as many heroes as doors).
In this level a hero standing above layer 0 (for example on a wall) may step out over water. From layer 0, heroes still refuse to step into water.

  trader #1 at (18,21), group 1

  magic mirror #2 at (16,23), facing south (reflects toward north and west)
