  o  boulder

Heroes:
  trader #6 at (28,14), group 1

  magic mirror #0 at (23,20), facing west (reflects toward east and north)
  boulder #1 at (24,19)
  boulder #2 at (21,21)
  boulder #4 at (25,12)
  boulder #5 at (27,13)
